Watch 01_blockout/previs_512x896_124f.mp4. Use this 124-frame file for H3, not the original 120-frame previz_720x1280.mp4. Open 01_blockout/scene.blend only when you're ready to change the camera later.

3. Use the local node template

In ComfyUI, open Templates, choose Video, and look for the local MiniMax H3 reference-to-video graph. The included 04_comfyui/rooftop_h3_ref2va_workflow.json is an API-format graph, not a ready-to-drag visual editor layout. Use it as the node/settings reference.

Upload and select your own copies of the four input files. The graph's old ch2/... paths will not exist on your machine. START_HERE.md lists the input nodes and their order.

4. Keep the baseline settings

Paste the full 03_prompts/h3_prompt.txt into the reference-to-video node. Keep its timing and Picture 1 / 2 / 3 / Video 1 order.

Run and save the MP4. Matching settings do not guarantee an identical shot on another installation.

5. Know when you're done

  • Your MP4 plays from start to finish, with sound.
  • You can see the sprint, jump, landing, rise and turn in order.
  • The camera follows the intended beats without a surprise cut or teleport.
  • The courier stays coherent through the full move.
  • You listened to the rain, steps and landing, and noted anything that does not fit.

Tick the downloaded completion checklist. If something fails, write down the exact error or time in the clip. Change one thing at a time.
