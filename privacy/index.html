Privacy

The short version

This site has no accounts, no comments and no forms. We don't sell or share personal data.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages help them most, and where they came from (for example YouTube or Instagram). Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. Google Analytics may set cookies. If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google Analytics cookies stay off until you say yes in the small banner, and you can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Google's own policy: How Google uses data from sites that use its services. To opt out of Google Analytics on every site, use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Downloads and purchases

Free recipes and ebooks are delivered by Gumroad. When you get one, Gumroad handles your email and payment under Gumroad's privacy policy. We see the email you give there so we can send your files and occasional updates, and you can unsubscribe from any email.
